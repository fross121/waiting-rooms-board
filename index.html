Tools

Right-drag erases. Hold Shift to paint a straight run. 1-4 pick a layer. Drag on the tile sheet to pick a block of tiles at once.

Rules of a room

  • One room per slot (16 by 24 cells, the dotted boxes). At least 10 cells.
  • Floor art on the storey's floor row with a cap at each end.
  • A doorway frame on any edge that joins another room; an end wall where the level stops.
  • Stairs make a stair room, which links storeys.
  • Base on the first layer, props on Decoration, near walls and things in front of the player on Foreground, exit signs on Signs.
  • Chests, backpacks and lockers painted from their sheets come alive in the game.
